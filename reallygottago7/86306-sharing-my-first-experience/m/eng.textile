Sharing My First Experience

Author: reallygottago7

*000*2023-02-17 15:58

Thank you to those of you who commented on my introduction a few weeks ago. I've been working up the courage to begin sharing here. It's time.

I'll begin by sharing an experience I had around 8-9 years ago. In the actual moment, the experience was horrifying. It wasn't until later I began to realize it might actually be something.

This took place during an NFL playoff game. I recall it being a late afternoon game as we had been tailgating from the morning into the afternoon. I do enjoy my beer. Not to the level of getting hammered and drunk (it takes a bit to get me there), but just enjoying a beer every 30 minutes or so as we tailgate. For anyone who drinks beer, you understand there will be the inevitable 'breaking of the seal'. I usually hold off as long as I can, because once you start going, you need to go every hour. 

At the stadium we go to, we tend to tailgate in the same lot each time. It has a row of porta johns in it. I know enough to know as we get closer to the game, the lines get longer. I'm a complete planner in my work and personal life. I'm always thinking ahead and anticipating potential problems. I know there are certain people who will say - you are a guy. You can just go whenever and wherever you want to. I'm not like that. I haven't ever been comfortable with other people possibly seeing me go. So, I wait it out in the porta john lines for my turn.

On this particular game, it was a playoff game. I'm a complete guy in that I don't like to miss any of the game. I'm in my seat for kickoff and try to hit the restrooms in between quarters or halftime. The game was really close heading into halftime. My bladder was screaming at me to go before halftime and beat the crowds like I normally do, but our team had the ball and were about to score. I stayed for the touchdown right before the half and then hurried for the nearest men's.

By the time I made it to the men's.. the line was already backed up into the hallway. When I saw the line.. there is that inner panic feeling when your stomach just drops and I mumbled 'Oh fuck...' as I saw it. I joined the line and immediately began praying for it to move as quickly as possible. I was kicking myself for waiting as long as I did. I know better than to let it get to the point it had or to possibly get caught in a line like this. I was completely bursting.

I will share I consider myself to be very good at holding it. I've been caught in similar moments before and have always made it, so my confidence was high. But this time felt much different. Anyone who knows how it goes after breaking the seal.. you need to go much more often, and the intensity is much higher.

Just joining the line and it barely moving after a few minutes.. my mind began to race. I was having a complete 'God, please don't let this be happening to me right now' moment.

I'm also the type I don't like others knowing I am ever struggling in this way. I do all I can to appear calm on the outside, even if I'm not.

I tried to distract myself by checking scores or looking at my phone. But it was already at the point where I couldn't think about anything else other than how badly I needed to go.

It's a big step for me just to start sharing about this experience and not knowing who will be reading it, even if you don't know who I am. I'm almost nervous as I type it all.

Who wants me to keep going?
