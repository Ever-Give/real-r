Opening Up About My Own Experiences

Author: reallygottago7

*000*2023-02-08 15:38

Making a post like this is a big, big step for me.

I've been reading posts and watching videos here for the last few years. It's been long enough to know I am among like minded people who would 'get me'. If any of you are like, you may have had a desperate experience and felt something different afterward. Maybe you internalized it, and if like me you felt weird because how it made you feel. Like.. did that really turn me on in some way? But, its not something you can talk about with anyone you know. So, you internalize it.

But then it happens again. You are caught in a completely desperate moment and you feel those same feelings all over. The more times it happens, the more you start to experiment with it privately at home. Then maybe you start to google search and discover. That is what brought me here.

About me, I'm a 44 year old married man with a family. I'm rather successful in my career. I'm a planner. I'm always on time, if not early for meetings. I keep a schedule. I anticipate problems well before they happen. People come to me for solutions tens of times per day and rarely if ever will you see me panicking. I am the calm for so many others.

Because of who I am, I feel I am the last person you would expect to see at the wrong end of a long men's line, completely panicking internally on if I will make it in time or not. Yet, I am an avid beer drinker (who is actually in great shape), coffee and water drinker, and I attend many, many sporting events and concerts.. which is where I have had most if not all of my unplanned experiences.

If you all could make me feel welcome and comfortable, I suppose I could begin by opening up about the first completely desperate close call I had at a football game about 8 years ago.

Thank you to the many of you who have already 'broken the ice' and been so vulnerable in posting and sharing here. It's made me feel like I can finally take this rather large step.

Brett
